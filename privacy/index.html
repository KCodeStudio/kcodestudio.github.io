1. Privacy policy for each game

Each of our games has its own privacy policy, because the games use different features – for example a different advertising setup. Please open the policy of the game you play:

  • Penguin to the Moon Privacy Policy

    Optional rewarded video with Google Limited Ads for every player · optional online leaderboards via Google Play Games

  • Floatstone Privacy Policy

    One optional rewarded video · Google consent form (UMP) where consent is required

  • Vinora Privacy Policy

    Full-screen ads after every fifth crash and an optional rewarded video · Google consent form (UMP) where consent is required

None of our games has user accounts of its own, in-app purchases or analytics of its own, and K-Code Studio does not run a server that receives data from the games.

3. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond and handle your request. The support address uses Gmail, so Google also processes email as the mailbox provider. Parents or guardians may contact us on behalf of younger players. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information, passwords or verification codes.

Support correspondence is retained for 90 days after the request has been finally resolved and is then deleted, unless an applicable legal obligation requires longer retention. Any required longer retention is limited to the relevant information and legally required duration. This is a mailbox procedure; the game does not automatically delete emails.

4. Your rights

Players, parents and guardians can contact kcodestudio.support@gmail.com about our games, this website and data processing. Details about each game are in its own privacy policy listed in section 1.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may complain to a competent data-protection authority, including the authority where you habitually reside or work in the EU.

For the publisher in Baden-Württemberg, Germany, the state authority is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg:
Contact the data-protection authority

5. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes even if they are not signed in. This hosting processing is separate from the game's local storage and its Google integrations.

Our static pages add no analytics scripts, cookies, advertising embeds, web forms or externally loaded fonts. GitHub's own infrastructure processing still applies. Email links open your email application; external links take you to the named provider's website.
