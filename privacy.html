2. Scope and audience

This privacy policy covers this website and Penguin to the Moon, an English-language Android game intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. The game is not directed at children under 13. This statement describes the intended audience; the game does not verify players' ages.

The main game can be played without signing in. Optional Google Play Games sign-in is required to view online leaderboards and submit scores. Section 10 explains this feature. The game has no chat or purchases with real money. Points, equipment and skins are virtual game progress.

A future game is covered by this policy only after it is identified here and any different data practices are described. Website hosting and support are explained separately from in-game processing.

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores scores, reached heights, points, rewards, owned and selected equipment and skins, and audio settings in private app storage. Local recovery copies may also be stored there. Android cloud backup of this game is disabled. K-Code Studio does not operate a server that receives this local save file.

The online leaderboard feature also caches information in private app storage, including player identifiers, display names, leaderboard entries, scores and ranks. Cached results help the game display previously loaded information.

Local data can be removed by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This does not delete scores or other data already stored by Google, or support emails you have sent.

4. Google advertising and messaging services

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform through the Poing Studios Godot plugin. It offers an optional rewarded video for revival after dying, when an ad is available. There are no banner ads or automatically displayed full-screen ads in the game.

The current development configuration uses Google's demo rewarded-ad unit. Test advertising can still involve communication with Google's services.

The advertising adapter starts when the game opens. It requests messaging and consent status and, when its conditions permit, initializes Google Mobile Ads and preloads an ad. Communication can therefore begin before the player chooses to watch a video.

The current configuration applies Google's child-directed treatment setting, a maximum requested ad content rating of G, and non-personalized ad requests to all players. The messaging request is marked as under the age of consent. These are technical settings currently applied regardless of a player's actual age. They do not mean that the game verifies age or targets children under 13. The app does not request Android advertising-ID or location permissions.

These settings do not prevent all SDK data processing. Choosing to watch a video is not, by itself, consent to all advertising-related data processing. Google's services may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information and permitted device-related identifiers. Processing depends on the SDK, configuration and service behavior.

The advertising integration does not send the game's local save file to K-Code Studio. Information used by the separate Google Play Games integration is described in section 10.

The integration uses Google Mobile Ads without mediation. Google describes SDK traffic as encrypted in transit. Non-personalized advertising does not, by itself, establish that consent is unnecessary. The applicable legal basis and any required consent for advertising-related processing remain under review and must be resolved before enabling live advertising for public release.

5. Google recipients, retention and transfers

The advertising and Play Games features communicate with Google. Google's services process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Google identifies ordinary AdMob as a Controller Service; its Controller Terms identify Google Ireland Limited as the Google end controller for European Controller Personal Data. The terms applicable to this publisher account and its advertising configuration still need to be checked before enabling live advertising.

Google determines retention according to data type, processing purpose, security and legal requirements. Uninstalling the game does not automatically erase data already received by Google. Google's privacy information explains retention and international processing, including applicable transfer safeguards.

The 90-day support-email retention period in section 6 does not apply to Google's service records or online scores. Play Games deletion options are described in section 10.

6. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond and handle your request. The support address uses Gmail, so Google also processes email as the mailbox provider. Parents or guardians may contact us on behalf of younger players. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information, passwords or verification codes.

Support correspondence is retained for 90 days after the request has been finally resolved and is then deleted, unless an applicable legal obligation requires longer retention. Any required longer retention is limited to the relevant information and legally required duration. This is a mailbox procedure; the game does not automatically delete emails.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

Players, parents and guardians can contact kcodestudio.support@gmail.com about the game and data processing. You can play the main game without signing in and decline optional rewarded videos. These choices do not necessarily stop all Google SDK initialization or service communication.

You can remove local data through Android's app-storage settings. For Google Play Games data, see section 10. We cannot identify an individual Google advertising SDK record from a local save alone; requests concerning Google's records may need to be directed to Google.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may complain to a competent data-protection authority, including the authority where you habitually reside or work in the EU.

10. Google Play Games and leaderboards

The game integrates Google Play Games Services for optional online leaderboards. It does not create a separate K-Code Studio player account. The main game does not require sign-in. Viewing online leaderboards and submitting online scores require authentication through Google Play Games. Depending on your Google Play Games settings, authentication may occur automatically.

The game uses the authenticated player's game-specific player identifier and display name. It submits reached heights and game scores to Google's leaderboards and retrieves leaderboard entries, display names, scores and ranks for display in the game. The game's own sign-in integration does not request your email address or Google password. Authentication is handled through Google's services.

Leaderboard information is processed by Google to operate the online ranking feature. Your display name and scores may be visible to other players, depending on Google's profile visibility settings and service rules. Avoid using a real name or other personal information as a public player name.

Google states that Play Games Services also collects service analytics and diagnostic information. Its services may process account, device and connection information as described in Google's privacy information. Google describes Play Games Services data as encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Some retrieved leaderboard information is cached on your device as described in section 3. Clearing local app storage does not delete the corresponding online data held by Google.

You can manage your Play Games profile visibility and sign-in settings through Google Play Games. Google provides options to delete Play Games data for an individual game or delete your Play Games profile. Account eligibility and the availability of features can depend on age, country and parental supervision. The game does not provide a way to bypass Google's account restrictions.
