2. Scope and games covered

This shared privacy policy covers this website and Penguin to the Moon, an English-language Android game intended for children aged 6–8. It has no player accounts, chat, public leaderboards or purchases with real money. Points, equipment and skins are virtual game progress.

This policy lists the games it covers so that one maintained page can describe their shared practices. A future game is covered only after it is added to this scope and any different data practices are described here. Website hosting and support are explained separately from in-game processing.

3. Progress on your device

The game stores scores, reached heights, points, rewards, owned and selected equipment and skins, and audio settings in private app storage. K-Code Studio does not operate a server that receives this save file. Local recovery copies may also be stored there. Android cloud backup of this game is disabled.

Progress remains until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Clearing storage removes local recovery copies as well. This does not delete data already received by Google or support emails you have sent.

4. Google advertising and messaging services

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform through the Poing Studios Godot plugin. It offers one voluntary rewarded video per flight for a revival, when an ad is available. There are no banner ads or automatically displayed full-screen ads in the game.

The advertising adapter starts when the game opens. It requests messaging/consent status and, when its conditions permit, initializes Google Mobile Ads and preloads an ad. Communication can therefore begin before the player taps Watch video.

Before advertising initialization, the app sets child-directed treatment, a maximum requested ad content rating of G, and non-personalized ad requests. The messaging request is marked as under the age of consent. Penguin to the Moon does not request Android advertising-ID or location permissions. These settings do not prevent all SDK data processing. Choosing Watch video is not treated as parental consent.

Google's services may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app/ad interactions, diagnostic information and permitted device-related identifiers. What is processed depends on the SDK, child-treatment settings and service behavior. K-Code Studio does not request a child's name, email address or precise location through the game and does not receive an identifiable player-data report or local save file through this integration.

The integration uses Google Mobile Ads without mediation. Google describes SDK traffic as encrypted in transit. Non-personalized advertising does not, by itself, establish that consent is unnecessary. The applicable legal basis and any required parental authorization for advertising-related processing remain under review. These requirements must be resolved before enabling advertising for a public child-directed release; this policy does not itself establish consent or parental authorization.

5. Google recipients, retention and transfers

Google identifies ordinary AdMob as a Controller Service. Its applicable Controller Terms describe the parties as independent controllers and identify Google Ireland Limited as the Google end controller for European Controller Personal Data. The terms applicable to this publisher account and the precise child-directed processing still need to be checked before public release.

Google determines retention according to data type, processing purpose, security and legal requirements. Uninstalling the game does not automatically erase data already received by Google. Google's privacy information explains access and deletion options. Its published transfer safeguards include applicable adequacy mechanisms, the Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. The 90-day support-mail rule below does not apply to Google's SDK records.

https://business.safety.google/adscontrollerterms/
https://business.safety.google/adsdatatransfers/
https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention

6. Contacting support

If a parent or guardian emails us, we receive the sender's email address and message and use them to respond and handle the request. The support address uses Gmail, so Google also processes email as the mailbox provider. Please avoid including children's personal information or unnecessary sensitive details.

Support correspondence is retained for 90 days after the request has been finally resolved and is then deleted, unless an applicable legal obligation requires longer retention. Any required longer retention is limited to the relevant information and legally required duration. This is a mailbox procedure; the game does not automatically delete emails.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

Parents or guardians can contact kcodestudio.support@gmail.com about the game and data processing. You can remove local progress through Android's app-storage settings. We cannot identify an individual Google SDK record from a local save alone; requests concerning Google's records may need to be directed to Google.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may complain to a competent data-protection authority, including the authority where you habitually reside or work in the EU. For the publisher in Baden-Württemberg, Germany, the state authority is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg:
https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/kontakt-aufnehmen/

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes even if they are not signed in. This hosting processing is separate from the game's local save and advertising integration. GitHub's privacy statement explains its processing, retention criteria, international transfers and privacy requests.

Our static page adds no analytics scripts, advertising embeds, web forms or externally loaded fonts. GitHub's own infrastructure processing still applies. Email links open your email application; other external links take you to the named provider's website.

https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
