ABOUT THE STUDIO

Simple to pick up.
Something to come back to.

K-Code Studio is a small independent developer brand from Germany. We build arcade games for Android that you understand in seconds – and that keep pulling you back for one more run.

  • ✦

    What we make

    Quick arcade games with one-finger controls, short runs and a clear goal: fly higher, fall deeper, get further than last time.

  • ○

    How we work

    Every game is played by testers before release. Their feedback on controls, difficulty and performance shapes what ends up on Google Play.

  • +

    Fair to players

    Our games are free to play. No pay-to-win and no tracking of our own. Ads stay short or optional, and our privacy policies explain them openly.
